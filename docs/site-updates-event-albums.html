Event albums

One private album per event. Guests send Reason their photos from the night, the host releases the album when it's over, and everyone who was there gets the photos in one place.

How it works

  • Send photos, name the event. Reason only collects photos you say are for an event. The first time, it tells you how they'll be shared and for how long, and waits for your yes.
  • Shared by you. Every photo shows the name on the guest list, never a phone number. Take your own photos out any time.
  • The host releases it. Only when the host ends the event. Everyone the host checked in gets one text with the link. Nothing else buzzes.
  • Highlights on top. The host can pin a few photos above the rest. Optional.
  • Save one, or the lot. Save any photo, or download the whole album.
  • Three days, then gone. The album shows its exact expiry. After it, the photos are deleted from Reason. What you saved stays on your phone.

Privacy, in plain words

  • Only people who were there. The host's check-in list is the only way in. Joining the event isn't the same as being there.
  • A link alone opens nothing. Reason checks who you are on every open; a forwarded link shows the other person only what they're allowed, or nothing.
  • Anyone can save a copy. Private access can't stop a saved photo being shared on, so Reason says so before you add anything.
